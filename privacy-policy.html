🫧 Bubble Sort — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

✅ This app collects no personal data of any kind.

Who we are

Bubble Sort is an independently developed mobile game designed for children, including children with autism and learning disabilities.

What information we collect

None. Bubble Sort does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. No names, no location, no device identifiers, no analytics, no tracking whatsoever.

Local device storage

The app saves your game progress (level number and star count) locally on your device only. This data never leaves the device and is not accessible to us or anyone else.

Children's privacy

Bubble Sort is designed for children. The app fully complies with COPPA (USA) and GDPR (UK/EU). No data is collected from users of any age.

Advertising

Bubble Sort contains no advertising of any kind.

In-app purchases

The current version contains no in-app purchases. The Star Store uses only stars earned through gameplay.

Third-party services

Bubble Sort loads the Nunito font from Google Fonts when an internet connection is available. No other third-party services are used.

Questions? Email: bubblesortapp@gmail.com
